SECTION 3: MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATIONS
The theoretical underpinnings of extended context processing involve complex mathematical relationships governing attention mechanisms and positional encoding schemes.

Attention computation formula:
Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QK^T/√d_k)V

Where:
- Q represents query matrices of dimension d_k
- K represents key matrices of dimension d_k  
- V represents value matrices of dimension d_v
- Scaling factor √d_k prevents softmax saturation

RoPE positional encoding formula:
f(x_m, m) = x_m * cos(mθ) + x̂_m * sin(mθ)

Where:
- x_m represents input embeddings at position m
- θ = 10000^(-2i/d) for dimension i
- x̂_m represents the rotated counterpart of x_m

YARN scaling modification:
θ_scaled = θ * (α * (L/L_train))^(-β)

Where:
- α represents the scaling factor (4.0 in our configuration)
- L represents current sequence length
- L_train represents original training length (32768)
- β controls interpolation behavior
